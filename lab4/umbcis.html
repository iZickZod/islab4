Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BS IS)

The Information Systems program focuses on the integration of computing and business principles to prepare students for leadership roles in technology management and development.

Core Focus Areas

  • Database Management and Design
  • Application Development
  • Systems Analysis and Design
  • Information Security and Assurance
  • Data Analytics and Business Intelligence

Admissions Requirements

Students must complete foundational courses in Calculus, Discrete Structures, and introductory Programming with a minimum GPA before formally declaring the IS major.
